Meet Heather Fielding

One mission: protect and educate.

Heather is a California Certified Studio Teacher and Child Labor Compliance Professional certified through the California Department of Industrial Relations, Division of Labor Standards Enforcement.

She works collaboratively with producers, assistant directors, coordinators, parents and schools to keep young performers learning, supported and protected throughout production.

Heather is an independent professional and is not an employee or representative of the State of California.
