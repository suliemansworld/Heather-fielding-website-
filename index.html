What I Do

Supporting Productions. Protecting Minors. Ensuring Compliance.

I provide dual-certified services that keep your production running smoothly while prioritizing education,
safety, and California labor law compliance for minors.

Studio Teacher

I provide on-set academic instruction tailored to each student’s needs, ensuring they stay on track with their education while working in the entertainment industry.

Child Labor
Compliance

Certified by the State of California Department of Industrial Relations Labor Law Enforcement. I help ensure productions follow all child labor laws and minor work permit regulations.

On-Set Advocate

I monitor and advocate for minors to ensure they receive required breaks, meals on time, proper rest, and are never placed in unsafe or inappropriate situations.

Production
Partner

I work collaboratively with producers, ADs, parents, and coordinators to create a positive, compliant, and productive environment for everyone.
